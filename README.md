# From Raw Data to Actionable Insights – Excel Capstone Project  

## 📊 Project Overview  
This project was completed as part of my Data Science training at *UTIVA Data School*.  
Using Excel (Pivot Tables, Slicers, Dashboards), I transformed raw *TNMT Retail Sales Data* into an interactive dashboard that answers key business questions.  

## 🔑 Key Business Questions  
- Which products drive the most revenue & profit?  
- Who are the top-performing sales teams?  
- How do revenue, population & median score relate across states?  
- What insights can guide smarter decision-making?  

## 📈 Deliverables  
- Revenue Trend Analysis  
- Products by Revenue & Contribution %  
- Sales Team Performance  
- State-Level Revenue vs Population & Median Score  
- Interactive Dashboard with embedded insights  

## 💡 Key Insights  
1. January’s top revenue drivers → *Vanities & Collectibles*  
2. *Nicholas Cunningham* led March sales for Outdoor Décor  
3. Revenue shows a *strong positive correlation* with population & median score

- Data Cleaning & Analysis
